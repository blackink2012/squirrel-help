One-line summary: the last two tabs in the settings window's left navigation. License activation decides whether this computer can use licensed features; About only holds software information, feedback and compliance.

Neither page is covered by the global lock: they work without ticking Unlock all settings (the activation entry must always be available).

How to open them

Settings window → click License activation or About in the left navigation (the tabs are stacked vertically).

FAQ

SymptomExplanation
Cannot find a "License" tabThe former License / About tab is now split into License activation and About, the last two items in the left navigation
The machine code is grayNormal: it is read-only; use Copy next to it
The activation key field is always visibleNormal: it sits under the machine code; enter a key and click Activate
Cannot find "My Account"It is now Account & my licenses on the License activation page; click Manage devices after signing in
Cannot find "Sync license"It is at the top right of Current license; it is also available for offline licenses (read-only registration)
Update check failsIt needs the network; the message states the reason, and you can always visit the website
Can I still open the library after expiry?Yes, browsing still works; import / export / convert require activation or renewal
