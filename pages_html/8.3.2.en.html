The chat surface can dock inside Maya or be handed to the system browser; both start the same dsh with the same sessions and tools, and only the window location differs.

Prerequisites

The AI assistant starts normally and dsh is installed.

Steps

  1. Open Settings → AI 设置 (AI settings).
  2. In the 「AI 对话面」 (AI chat surface) group find 「在 Maya 内嵌 AI 对话面（取消则用系统浏览器打开）」 (embed the AI chat surface in Maya; uncheck to open it in the system browser).
  3. Checked means it docks inside Maya next time; unchecked means the system browser.
  4. Close Settings and reopen AI 工具 (AI tools) → AI 助手 (AI assistant). The setting applies on the next click.

Verify the result

  • Embedded mode: a docked panel appears on the right of Maya with the scene name in its title.
  • Browser mode: the system browser opens the dsh page and Maya shows no docked panel; while the assistant runs, the 「AI 工具」 (AI tools) menu gains 「召回AI面板」 (Recall the AI panel).

Tips and warnings

  • When the Maya layout swallows the embedded panel, the plug-in recalls it automatically as long as the service is running; a panel you hid yourself is never forced back.
  • In browser mode the tab belongs to the browser and the plug-in cannot see you closing it; clicking the toggle then may stop the service, so use 「召回AI面板」 (Recall the AI panel) to recover.
  • When the embedded component is unavailable it falls back to the browser even if embedding is still checked.
  • The 「AI 工具」 (AI tools) menu is rebuilt per state: while the assistant is not running it contains only AI thumbnail analysis / AI search preview / AI assistant / stop the AI backend service; 「关闭 AI 助手」 (Close the AI assistant) and 「召回AI面板」 (Recall the AI panel) appear only while it runs (open the assistant once and look at the menu again).
