When on, dragging a model, dynamics, scene or light into the Maya viewport snaps the asset to the model surface under the cursor and aligns it to the normal.

Effect

It makes a dropped asset land on a visible surface in the viewport instead of returning to its original coordinates.

Values and default

  • The checkbox 「拖入资产（模型 / 动态 / 场景 / 灯光）时吸附到场景表面」 (snap assets dragged into the scene onto the surface) is on by default.

Scope

  • For models, dynamics and scenes: the asset's up axis (+Y) aligns with the surface normal and its bottom edge touches the drop point.
  • For lights: the light direction (−Z) aligns with the surface normal, shining outward from the surface with its back against it.
  • With several assets dropped together they line up along the surface; when the rules differ they import at their original positions.
  • It only applies to the models, dynamics, scenes and lights sub-libraries; material, texture and HDR drop semantics are unaffected.
  • When off, assets import at their original position, restoring the previous behavior.

When to change it

  • Keep it on to place assets quickly onto an existing model surface.
  • Turn it off for assets with complex transforms that must return to their original coordinates, or when no surface is available.
  • The setting is read at the next drag, so applying is enough and no restart is needed.

Tips

Tip: snapping needs an actual model face under the cursor; dropping over empty space falls back to the original position.

Tip: for more detail on drop points and rotation rules see "Snap to the surface".
