The wishlist is a cross-session "to look at" queue on the right of the AI search dialog; it is a single linear list and a separate organization tool from favorites.

Prerequisites

  • The AI search dialog is open and an asset library is loaded (adding an item resolves the asset ID into an entry).

Steps

  1. Right-click a card in the AI search results and choose 「加入心愿单」 (Add to wishlist).
  2. You can also drag the result card straight into the 「心愿单」 (Wishlist) panel on the right.
  3. Clicking an entry locates the matching asset in the main window.
  4. Click the close icon at the top right of an entry (tooltip 「从心愿单移除」 (Remove from wishlist)) to remove it.
  5. Click 「清空」 (Clear) at the top of the panel and confirm in 「确定清空全部心愿单吗？」 (Clear the whole wishlist?) to empty the queue.

Verify the result

  • The count next to the 「心愿单」 (Wishlist) title increases or decreases accordingly.
  • Closing and reopening the dialog, or even restarting the plug-in, keeps the entries (stored under the user setting key ai_search_wishlist).

Tips

  • Adding the same asset twice reports 「已在心愿单中：资产名」 (already in the wishlist: <asset name>).
  • The wishlist stores only display fields such as ID, name, path, sub-library, category and tags; it does not store a preview image.
  • It is completely independent of favorites: starring an asset does not change the wishlist, and the wishlist is not grouped by collection.
  • On the command side use wishlist: op=list/add/remove/clear, with dry_run; add reports existing and missing IDs honestly.
