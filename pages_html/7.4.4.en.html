Creating a dome light picks a template for the current renderer, builds a dome light and connects the selected HDR to it.

Prerequisites

  • The current renderer is Arnold, V-Ray or Redshift.
  • A usable HDR is available: an HDR asset in the library, or one in the plug-in's Assets/IBL directory.

Steps

  1. Open the 「创建资产」 (Create asset) dialog and click 「创建灯光」 (Create light) in the thumbnail source area.
  2. The menu lists the environment images (.hdr, .exr and so on) under Assets/IBL; hovering an entry shows a same-named PNG preview.
  3. Pick an HDR and the tool chooses a template for the current renderer: aiSkyDomeLight.ma for Arnold, VRayLightDome.ma for V-Ray and rsDomeLight.ma for Redshift.
  4. You can also double-click an HDR asset in the library or drag it into the viewport; the same template logic applies.
  5. After creation adjust rotation, intensity and exposure in the attribute editor as needed.

Verify the result

A dome light appears in the Outliner and is selected; the attribute editor shows the HDR connection (Arnold's file, V-Ray's VRayPlaceEnvTex or Redshift's tex0 path); the render background shows environment lighting.

Tips

  • The template directory is Assets/HDR_ligt (containing aiSkyDomeLight.ma, VRayLightDome.ma and rsDomeLight.ma); the default template can be changed per sub-library in Settings → 右键菜单 (Context menu), in the double-click command area in the lower half of the right column, and dragging an HDR follows it.
  • The created light locks visibility to prevent accidental hiding; to switch it off temporarily change intensity or exposure rather than unlocking visibility.
  • Arnold sets camera=0, Redshift sets cameraRayContributionScale=0 and V-Ray sets domeSpherical=0, so the render background keeps its alpha while lighting and reflections still work.

Warnings

Only Arnold, V-Ray and Redshift can create a dome light automatically today; the native Maya renderer does not.
