One-line summary: the "A › B › C" line in the middle of the asset area is the path of where you are — click an earlier segment to jump back, and the last segment marks your current position (not clickable).

Where it is

In the middle of the asset area toolbar, right of Create asset and left of the result count. It is always shown and needs no opening; it changes when you click a category, enter favorites, or open an asset's versions / LODs.

What each segment represents

SegmentExampleDescription
Library root / All assetsAll assets › Materials › MetalThe first part is the library root; All assets means across every sub-library
Sub-library rootMaterials, Models, Lights…The current sub-library
Category / sub-categoryMetal › CopperThe category level you clicked into, possibly several
Asset nameMetal › Copper › openpbr_Aluminum_BrushedAn extra segment after you double-click an asset to enter its asset scope
Version / variant / LOD… › openpbr_Aluminum_Brushed › Versions › LODExtra segments added while browsing versions / variants / LODs inside an asset

Behavior of each segment

ActionEffectNotes
Hover a clickable segmentThe text underlines and the cursor becomes a handOnly non-current segments are clickable
Click an earlier category segmentJumps back to that category and refilters the grid by itSame effect as clicking that category in the left tree
Click the segment before the asset nameLeaves the asset scope and returns to the category's asset listThis is the way "out of an asset's version view"
Click the version / variant segmentSwitches between the version list and the variant listOnly appears once you are in the matching scope
Click the last segment (current scope)Nothing happens (bold, unclickable)The last segment always marks where you are

What happens when space runs out

On a narrow window the breadcrumb protects the tail (your current position is always visible) and compresses / omits earlier segments from left to right; when clipped, an "…" appears at the far left. Widen the window or collapse the side bars and the full path shows again.

Submenus

The breadcrumb is not a menu and has no dropdown or submenu; clicking a segment jumps directly.

Beginner pitfalls

  • Looking for a back button to go up one level: clicking the previous segment in the breadcrumb is faster.
  • The path starts with "…": normal; it means outer levels were omitted. Widen the window to see them.
  • "The list disappeared" after double-clicking an asset: you entered that asset's asset scope (version / LOD views); click a category segment or the segment before the asset name to return to the list.
  • The breadcrumb disagrees with the left tree: trust the breadcrumb — it reflects what the main area really shows (with several categories multi-selected on the left, the grid shows their union).
