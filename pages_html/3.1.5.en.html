「属性」 (Properties) sits at the top right and 「预览」 (Preview) below it: the first reads and writes asset metadata, the second views and switches images and fills in thumbnails.

Properties panel

The properties panel shows the selected asset's display name, asset name, node type, asset type, category, format, animation format, size, resolution, software, renderer, color space, creation time, tags and notes. With no asset selected it shows 「未选择材质」 (no material selected) and every field is -.

  • Click the edit button in the title row to enter edit mode: change the name, category, tags, notes and so on, then click 「保存」 (Save) to apply or 「取消」 (Cancel) to discard.
  • Notes can be edited by double-clicking; for tags click 「+ 添加标签」 (+ Add tag) and pick a common tag or type a new one.
  • The favorite button adds the asset to or removes it from favorites.

Preview panel

  • Shows the thumbnail of the current asset; when there are several, switch with the mouse wheel or the floating arrows on either side of the image.
  • Double-click the preview to open the asset details / full-size preview.
  • The buttons below offer 「截取」 (Capture), 「导入」 (Import), 「添加」 (Add) and 「截取添加」 (Capture and add); with several images there is also a counter and 「删除」 (Delete).
  • Right-click the preview for the 「更新缩略图」 (Update thumbnail) menu: capture / import / Maya playblast / render / add / capture and add; for materials, models, lights, scenes, dynamics, textures and node trees there is also 「后台渲染」 (Background render), a headless offscreen render.

Verify the result

  • Selecting a different card in the grid switches the property fields and the preview image together.
  • After changing the display name and saving, the name on the card updates immediately.

Tips

  • Rename assets and change categories and tags in these panels or the context menu; do not rename .zasset folders by hand.
  • Properties and preview are two independent areas and can be moved, split or closed separately (see 3.2).
