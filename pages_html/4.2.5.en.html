The category tree expands level by level, collapses as a whole, and automatically expands parents and scrolls into place when an asset is located from search or the wishlist.

Expanding and collapsing

  • Click the arrow in front of a category row to expand or collapse that node.
  • Double-clicking a node with sub-categories also expands / collapses it (the native double-click is disabled and the tree decides, avoiding a double toggle under Maya).
  • Double-clicking 「全部资产」 (All assets) expands every top-level category at once; double-clicking again collapses them.
  • Right-clicking a sub-library root node or top-level category node: the bottom of the menu holds 「全部展开」 (Expand all) and 「全部折叠」 (Collapse all); the context menu of an ordinary category node only offers add sub-category, open folder, move to, edit and delete, without expand / collapse.

Locating

  • Ordinary locating: clicking a category selects it and loads its assets; expanding the tree never changes the selection.
  • Clicking 「定位」 (Locate) from AI search or the wishlist: the plug-in switches to the asset's sub-library, expands the parent categories level by level, selects the category and scrolls the asset into view in the grid.
  • Pressing Ctrl+F inside the grid scrolls the selected card to the center of the viewport.

Verify the result

When the category tree refreshes (for example after creating, deleting or moving a category), the expansion state and the current selection are kept: previously expanded nodes stay expanded and the selected category stays where it was. After a plug-in restart the previous expansions and selection are restored as well.
