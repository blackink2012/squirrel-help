The dependency scan walks the scene nodes during export or import to collect textures, caches, proxies and referenced files; missing items are recorded in the asset's meta.json.

Prerequisites

  • The objects are in the scene and cache / proxy nodes are connected.
  • 「收集关联文件」 (Collect linked files) is checked on export; importing external files also runs a text-level collection.

Steps

  1. Check 「收集关联文件」 (Collect linked files) in the export dialog.
  2. The scan runs automatically during export; it is read-only and does not modify the scene.
  3. Open <asset>.zasset/meta.json and read dependencies_missing, dependencies_out_of_scope, dependencies_unresolved_tokens and dependency_scan.
  4. Put missing files back at their original paths and export again.

What is scanned

LayerNotes
Native registryThe broken-link list from filePathEditor
Attribute scanlistAttr(usedAsFilename=True), not limited to a node allowlist
Special node patchesAttributes of Bifrost, Yeti, nCache, file sequences and similar
Expansion and classificationFrame sequences and UDIM expanded and classified by extension
Collected directoriesdeps/ sub-directories such as textures, caches, proxies, volumes, references, audio

Verify the result

  • dependencies_missing is empty in meta.json.
  • deps/ contains every needed file.
  • The export log says 「依赖扫描: 命中 N 个源文件…缺失 0」 (dependency scan: N source files matched… 0 missing).

Tips

  • Out-of-scope dependencies are recorded in dependencies_out_of_scope and are not packaged.
  • Paths containing <MAYA_RESOURCES> land in dependencies_unresolved_tokens and need manual handling.
  • Library-wide batch maintenance lives in the 「维护」 (Maintenance) menu of the top toolbar: 「补缺失缩略图…」 (Fill in missing thumbnails…) scans the whole library for missing or placeholder thumbnails (15.12.8), 「清理无效资产」 (Clean invalid assets) finds entries without meta.json or empty shells (15.12.9), and 「重建资产索引」 (Rebuild asset index) rescans the list (4.12.2).
