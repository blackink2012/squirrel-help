Splitting divides one area in two, while merging hands the current area's space to a neighboring area.

Splitting an area

  1. Click the function button on the right of the target area's header.
  2. Choose a direction in the second column: 「向右拆分」 (Split right), 「向下拆分」 (Split down), 「向左拆分」 (Split left), 「向上拆分」 (Split up).
  3. The original and the new area each take half, and the new area shows the same panel type as the original.
  4. To change its content, switch the panel type in the new area's header (see 3.2.6).

Merging an area

  1. Click the function button of the area that should disappear.
  2. Choose 「并入…」 (Merge into…) and then a direction (up / down / left / right).
  3. That area closes and its space goes to the neighbor in that direction; a direction without a neighbor is not shown.

If you only want to remove an area, choose 「关闭本区域」 (Close this area) instead; the space is distributed among sibling neighbors.

Verify the result

  • After splitting the area count increases and two panels of the same type appear, each working independently.
  • After merging the area count decreases, the neighbor grows and the original area's content is gone.
  • Reopening the window keeps the split and merge results (the layout is saved automatically).

Tips

  • A newly split area holds its own independent panel instance and does not compete with the original; the same panel type can appear in several areas at once.
  • At least one area must remain; when only one is left it can no longer be closed or merged.
