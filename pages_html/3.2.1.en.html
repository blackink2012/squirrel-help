An area can dock inside the main window or be dragged out into a separate window; drag a floating window back to the edge of the main window to dock it again.

Docking and moving

  1. Hold the blank space in the area header (next to the panel name).
  2. Drag to another position inside the main window; docking indicators appear while dragging.
  3. Release and the area docks to the indicated edge or column.

Dragging the gap between areas changes width and height, and the proportions are saved automatically.

Floating and tearing off

  1. Click the function button on the right of the area header to open the area menu.
  2. Choose 「撕下」 (Tear off) to turn the current area into a separate window, or 「撕下副本」 (Tear off a copy) to duplicate the same panel type first and float the copy, leaving the original in the layout.
  3. You can also drag the area header out of the main window and release to create a floating window.
  4. Floating windows move and resize freely; drag one back to the edge of the main window to dock it again, or choose 「停靠回主窗口」 (Dock back into the main window) in the area menu.

Closing an area

  • Area menu → 「关闭本区域」 (Close this area).
  • At least one area always remains; closing the last one is refused with 「至少要保留一个区域」 (at least one area must remain) in the status bar.

Verify the result

  • Tearing off creates a separate window and the freed space in the main window goes to neighboring areas.
  • Dragging the floating window back to the edge of the main window and releasing docks it again.
  • Closing an area reduces the count, and the menu offers creating a new area.

Tips

  • With the layout locked you cannot drag areas around or pull them out, but 「撕下」 (Tear off) in the area menu still works (see 3.2.4).
  • Floating state is not kept for the next start: while an area floats the layout tree may not be readable, so by design the plug-in saves nothing and returns to a docked layout after restart; the docked layout, proportions and maximized state are saved automatically (see 3.2.5).
