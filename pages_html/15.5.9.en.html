One-line summary: right-clicking an images asset card in the asset grid opens this menu. Images are loose-file pictures, and there are four different ways into Maya — the top four items of this menu.

How to open it

  1. Left Category tab → click Images or any category under it;
  2. In the center asset grid, right-click any image card.

The images menu is the shortest one: no import, no add reference, no update thumbnail, no update asset, no format conversion, no preview node. The reason is simple — an image has no geometry and nothing besides its thumbnail to update.

Which of the four ways to choose

Your goalWhich item
Use this image as a texture on a materialAssign to selected material slot (with a material selected) or Create material (the textures sub-library route)
Just want an image texture node in the scene and wire it yourselfImport as file node
Want a reference image to line up against in the viewportCreate imagePlane
Just want to look at the image and confirm which one it isOpen image

Notes specific to this sub-library

  • Images have no "Update thumbnail": an image's thumbnail is the image itself, so replacing the picture means re-importing, not swapping a thumbnail.
  • An imagePlane hangs on the current viewport camera: switching cameras may hide it. That is Maya behavior, not the plug-in losing it.
  • "Assign to selected material slot" only targets the color slot: it does no type-based smart matching (that is the textures sub-library's Assign texture to material).
