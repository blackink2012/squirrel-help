The 「分类」 (Categories) tab, the category library view, mirrors the folders of the current main asset library: 「全部资产」 (All assets) at the top, then sub-libraries and categories level by level.

What the panel contains

  • Library dropdown at the top: switches the current asset library.
  • 「全部资产」 (All assets): shows every asset across all sub-libraries, counted as the sum of them; it is always shown and never affected by hiding settings.
  • Top-level nodes: the sub-libraries (materials, models, lights, textures, scenes, HDR, dynamics, images, audio, animation data, node trees — 11 built-in sub-libraries) plus custom top-level categories in the library root; a sub-library can be hidden with Settings → 子库与分类 → 显示在主 UI (Sub-libraries and categories → Show in the main UI) (the screenshot hides 「节点树」 (Node trees)).
  • The last row, 「脚本」 (Scripts), is the script library entry and not a sub-library; the 「回收站」 (Recycle bin) entry sits below it (see 15.3.4.4).
  • Counts: the number after a category name is the asset count of that category and all its descendants.

Actions

  • Clicking a category shows only the assets of that category and its sub-categories in the middle grid, and refreshes the properties / preview on the right.
  • Ctrl-click and Shift-click select several categories, and the grid shows the union of all selected categories (including their descendants); selections can span sub-libraries.
  • Right-clicking a category offers new sub-category, open folder, move, edit and delete; parent nodes also offer 「全部展开 / 全部折叠」 (Expand all / Collapse all).
  • When collapsed, the left column becomes a narrow icon strip that still switches top-level categories.

The tree order follows the folders on disk; after adding, moving or deleting a category the tree is rebuilt and keeps the previous expansion and selection where possible.
