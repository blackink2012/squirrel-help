One-line summary: selecting an asset in the asset grid lists all of its information fields in the Properties area on the right.

The properties panel is for looking only; to change something use the context menu (see 8.6.2) or the edit button in the title bar (see 8.6.3).

How to open it

  1. Click any category in the left navigation to enter the asset grid;
  2. Single-click any asset card (no double-click needed);
  3. The Properties area on the right immediately shows that asset's fields.

If the panel was closed, do not worry: the Panels menu at the top reopens the Properties area (see 8.2.5); the area can also be dragged, split and torn off (see 3.2.1).

When no asset is selected

  • The properties list collapses entirely while the title row and pencil button remain;
  • Right-clicking or clicking the pencil does nothing then (there is no object to edit).

Read-only state (recycle bin entries)

Selecting a record in the recycle bin makes both properties and preview read-only:

  • The star (favorite) and pencil (edit) buttons hide;
  • Edit in the context menu is gray, leaving only copy / copy ID;
  • The notes gain "moved to recycle bin · deletion time".
  • Recycle bin usage is in 8.8.3.
