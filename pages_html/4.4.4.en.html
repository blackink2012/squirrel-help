The 「预览」 (Preview) panel on the right shows the current asset's thumbnails and offers capture, import, add and switching between several images.

Interface

  • Preview area: shows the selected thumbnail; GIF/MP4 thumbnails animate.
  • When the mouse is inside and the asset has several thumbnails, arrows appear on both sides; the wheel also switches.
  • With several images, current / total and 「删除」 (Delete) appear below; Delete is available only for the second slot onwards, the main thumbnail cannot be deleted here.
  • Button row: 「截取」 (Capture), 「导入」 (Import), 「添加」 (Add), 「截取添加」 (Capture and add).

Actions

  1. Select an asset in the grid and the preview panel loads its thumbnail list automatically.
  2. 「截取」 (Capture) opens the screenshot tool to drag a region; 「导入」 (Import) picks a local image to replace the current one.
  3. 「添加」 (Add) imports an image as a new thumbnail slot; 「截取添加」 (Capture and add) writes a screenshot into a new slot.
  4. Right-clicking the preview opens the same 「更新缩略图」 (Update thumbnail) menu as the asset card (capture, import, Maya playblast, render, add, capture and add, background render).
  5. Double-clicking the preview opens the asset details; when the asset has several versions it opens the version browser.

Verify the result

Afterwards the preview refreshes immediately, the counter and arrow states follow with several images, and the thumbnail on the grid card updates too.

Tips

  • Multiple thumbnails cycle automatically (about every 2 seconds); hovering the preview pauses the cycle, and a playing video is not interrupted.
