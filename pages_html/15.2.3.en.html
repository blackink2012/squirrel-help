One-line summary: the capsule-shaped input in the middle of the toolbar is the search bar — typing is keyword search, and with the upload / screenshot / screen recording buttons on its left it means "take an image, a video or a sound and find similar assets".

How to open it

Nothing to open; it is always in the middle of the toolbar. Usage:

  1. Click into the input, type, and press Enter to search;
  2. Or click the icon buttons embedded on the left of the input (upload ▾ / screenshot / screen recording) for media search;
  3. The AI search checkbox sits on the right of the input and is ticked by default.

The placeholder text changes

The placeholder is not fixed; it reflects what pressing Enter will do right now:

Current stateRoughly what it says
AI search ticked (default)Describe the asset you want in one sentence, press Enter for AI search
AI search untickedSearch name, tag, ID...
In the Audio categoryDescribe the sound / music you want in one sentence

You can also drop files straight in

Drop an image / video / audio file onto the search box and it picks image search, video search or audio search from the actual file format, with no button needed first. You can also drop an asset card into the search box and use that card as the "similarity reference".

Beginner pitfalls

  • No icon buttons on the left of the input: AI search is not ticked. Media search depends on AI capabilities, so the buttons appear once it is on.
  • Image search finds nothing: first confirm the AI dependencies are installed (the AI page in settings has AI dependency check and one-click install, see 8.9.3); missing dependencies are reported directly.
  • Other assets vanished after searching: search results are isolated (only matches remain) by design. Click ✕ on the right of the input or clear the filter to restore them, see 4.1.5.5.
  • The search box is squeezed to a few characters: the window is too narrow, or the batch operation bar takes space after multi-selecting assets; widen the window and the search bar regains priority.
