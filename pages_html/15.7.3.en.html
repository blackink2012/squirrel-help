One-line summary: a row of four buttons below the preview area decides which image becomes this asset's thumbnail and whether extra images are stored.

Remember one dividing line and you cannot go wrong: Capture and Import replace the cover (main image); Add and Capture and add append new images and leave the cover alone.

When they are usable

All four share one condition and are all gray when it fails:

ConditionDescription
A normal asset is selectedAll four are gray with no asset selected
It is not a recycle bin entryThe recycle bin is read-only and all four are gray
It is not a version / variant virtual entryVirtual entries have no thumbnail of their own and all four are gray

A typical operation

Change the cover (most common)

  1. Select the asset so the preview shows an image;
  2. Click Capture, drag a box around the area you want as the cover, release;
  3. The preview and card cover immediately show the new image (scaled to the default thumbnail size).

Add an alternative image

  1. Click Add and pick local images (multi-select);
  2. A counter appears below the preview (such as 2/3); click ▶ or use the wheel to view;
  3. If you do not want it, switch to that image and click Delete.

Undo and risks

OperationUndoableHow to undo
Capture / import (overwrite the main image)NoCapture another or import another image; the old cover has no backup
Add / capture and add (new slots)Yes (delete the new image)Switch to the new image and click Delete; deleting itself is irreversible
Deleting an extra imageNoNo confirmation and no recycle bin (see 4.1.7.5)
警告

Capture and Import overwrite the main image directly and the plug-in offers no "undo the last thumbnail"; if the old cover matters, copy the asset directory's thumb.sicon in Explorer before operating.
