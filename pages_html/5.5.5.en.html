「指定贴图」 (Assign texture) connects a texture asset's image to the matching channel of the selected material, or connects an HDR to the selected dome light.

Prerequisites

  • The texture asset carries a properties record (describing what each texture is for).
  • Texture case: one or more material nodes are selected.
  • HDR case: a dome light is selected.

Steps

  1. Texture asset: select the material node, right-click the texture asset and choose 「指定贴图到材质」 (Assign texture to material).
  2. HDR asset: select the dome light, right-click the HDR asset and choose 「指定贴图」 (Assign texture).
  3. The plug-in takes the texture at the asset's default resolution and connects it, according to its PBR role, to the texture attribute of the target material or light.

Verify the result

  • The target material gains a file node whose fileTextureName points at the matching texture inside the asset.
  • The dome light's texture attribute points at the HDR file, with the Raw color space.
  • The Script Editor reports how many nodes were assigned.

Tips

  • With nothing selected the messages are 「未选中任何对象，请先选中一个材质节点」 (nothing selected; select a material node first) or 「请先选中一个穹顶灯」 (select a dome light first).
  • 「指定贴图」 (Assign texture) only changes connections and never creates material nodes; for a new material use 「创建材质」 (Create material).
  • Where textures and HDRs are stored follows the texture import policy.
