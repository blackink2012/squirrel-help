The AI assistant is a toggle: clicking while it is off starts dsh and shows the chat surface, clicking again while it is on closes the panel and ends the dsh process.

Prerequisites

The plug-in main window is open and dsh is installed; without a local dsh an install guide appears first (see 8.3.5).

Steps

  1. Click 「AI 工具」 (AI tools) in the toolbar and choose 「AI 助手」 (AI assistant).
  2. Wait for the state to change from 「正在启动 AI 助手…」 (Starting the AI assistant…) to 「关闭 AI 助手」 (Close the AI assistant). The first start takes a few seconds; when the main window opens the plug-in warms up in the background, so it is usually faster.
  3. Once the panel appears you can chat. If the panel is collapsed while the service still runs, open AI 工具 (AI tools) → 召回AI面板 (Recall the AI panel) to bring it back.
  4. To end the conversation click AI 工具 (AI tools) → 关闭 AI 助手 (Close the AI assistant), which closes the panel and ends the dsh process.

When the menu items appear

The 「AI 工具」 (AI tools) menu is rebuilt per state each time it opens, so the wording in the same position changes:

Assistant stateThat menu itemRelated items
Off (or closed)「AI 助手」 (AI assistant)Neither recall nor close appears
Starting「正在启动 AI 助手…」 (Starting the AI assistant…)Same as above; no chat surface to recall yet
On「关闭 AI 助手」 (Close the AI assistant)「召回AI面板」 (Recall the AI panel) appears as well

So "close is missing from the menu" usually means the assistant is not running — that is by design, not a missing item.

Verify the result

  • After opening: the menu item becomes 「关闭 AI 助手」 (Close the AI assistant) and a chat panel appears on the right of Maya, or the system browser opens the page.
  • After recalling: the panel reappears with the previous session intact.
  • After stopping: the menu returns to 「AI 助手」 (AI assistant) and Task Manager no longer shows the dsh process started this time.

Tips and warnings

  • Clicking again while it is starting cancels that start and never leaves a half-open panel.
  • Closing Maya also ends dsh, so no orphan process is left behind.
  • AI 工具 (AI tools) → 停止 AI 底座服务 (Stop the AI backend service) only stops the local backend and never closes Maya; the backend starts again the next time the assistant opens.
