The plug-in works with the renderers installed in Maya and does not bundle any renderer; material nodes, proxy formats and conversion depend on whether the matching plug-in is loaded.

Installed plug-in detection

  • It queries Maya's pluginInfo to see whether a plug-in is loaded, registered or unavailable; common plug-in names are mtoa (Arnold), vrayformaya (V-Ray) and redshift4maya (Redshift).
  • Results are reported as loaded, present but not loaded, or unavailable, and decide whether proxy import, material conversion and rendering entry points are enabled.
  • Capabilities for MASH, Bifrost and USD follow the plug-ins actually installed in Maya; this product does not ship them (the exact support scope is to be confirmed).

Recommendations

  • Confirm the target renderer is loaded before importing or converting, otherwise material nodes or proxy files may not be created.
  • Cross-renderer conversion only handles attributes covered by the mapping table; complex node networks, gradients and procedural textures need a manual review.
  • Proxy import relies on the matching renderer's stand-in or proxy nodes; without the plug-in you can only keep the file, not display it in the viewport.
