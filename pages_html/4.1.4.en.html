Favorites are persistent, per-library groups of bookmarked assets; the wishlist is a temporary cross-session "to look at" queue inside AI search. The two are unrelated.

Favorites

  • They live on the 「收藏」 (Favorites) tab in the left column; each library has its own set, so the list changes with the library, and the panel header names the current library (main asset library / project library).
  • A 「默认收藏夹」 (default collection) is built in; new collections can be renamed, emptied or deleted (the default collection can only be emptied).
  • The star at the bottom left of an asset card shows whether it belongs to the active collection; click the star to add or remove it. The data is written to favorites.json in the library root.

Wishlist

  • It sits on the right of the AI search dialog as a linear queue, independent of collections and libraries, and persists across sessions.
  • Right-click a card in the AI search results to add it to the wishlist, or drag the card into the wishlist panel; clicking an entry locates it in the main window, ✕ on an entry removes it, and the top right can clear the whole list.
  • The wishlist stores only the light fields needed to locate an asset; it is not a copy of the asset.

Which one to use

Long-term grouping and repeated use → favorites; just gathering a batch of search results to look at later → wishlist.
