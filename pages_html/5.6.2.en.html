With snapping on, dropped models, dynamics, scenes and lights land on the model surface under the cursor instead of at the origin.

Prerequisites

  • Settings → 常规 → 拖放 (General → Drag and drop) → 拖入资产（模型 / 动态 / 场景 / 灯光）时吸附到场景表面 (snap assets dragged into the scene onto the surface) is checked, which is the default.
  • A hittable mesh surface exists under the cursor.

Steps

  1. Drag a snappable asset to the target position in the viewport.
  2. Release.
  3. The plug-in casts a ray at the cursor position to get the hit point and normal.
  4. After importing, it places the new root node at the hit point: models, dynamics and scenes stand up along the normal and lights shine along it.
  5. For a multi-selection drop the groups line up side by side by average width, and lights keep a gap based on the hit distance.

Verify the result

  • The asset lands on the surface under the cursor, not at the world origin.
  • The model's local Y axis aligns with the surface normal, and the light's direction follows the normal.
  • The Script Editor prints [DragDrop] 已吸附到 <mesh> 表面 with the drop point and normal.

Tips

  • When there is no snappable surface under the cursor or the drop point is outside the viewport, the asset imports at its original position and is never lost.
  • A failed snap placement only falls back to "import at the original position"; the import itself still completes.
  • With snapping off, the drop position is the asset's original position and no drop preview is shown.
  • Images, audio, animation data and node trees do not snap: images and audio are not imported when dropped into the viewport, and animation data and node trees import straight into the scene (no placement and no drop preview).
  • A mixed selection with different placement rules does not snap, avoiding two orientations in one batch.
