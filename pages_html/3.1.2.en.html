The top toolbar doubles as the window title bar: collapse and search on the left, refresh, AI tools, quick tools, panels and layout, settings and help on the right.

From left to right

Search boxSearch by name, tag or ID; three icon buttons for upload, screenshot and screen recording sit on the left inside the box; the placeholder says "describe the asset you are looking for in one sentence, press Enter for AI search"

There is a blank drag area on each side of the search box; drag it to move the whole window. Every toolbar button is icon-only and shows its name and description on hover. You can also drop an image, video or audio file onto the search box to find similar assets by actual format.

Verify the result

  • Hovering any icon button shows a tooltip.
  • Clicking 「刷新」 (Refresh) reloads the asset grid; clicking 「帮助」 (Help) opens the help home page.

Tips

  • The 「AI 搜索」 (AI search) checkbox is on by default; unchecking it returns to plain keyword search and hides the upload / screenshot / screen-recording buttons inside the search box (media search depends on AI).
  • The batch operation bar appears only when several assets are selected and hides for a single selection or none.
