One-line summary: besides the toolbar buttons, the top row also holds two sidebar toggles and the window controls — they govern what the window looks like, and change nothing in the asset library.

How to open them

Nothing to open; they are always at the top of the window:

  1. Collapse / expand left sidebar: the fold icon at the far left of the toolbar, right of the squirrel icon.
  2. Collapse / expand right sidebar: the fold icon at the far right of the toolbar.
  3. Minimize / maximize / close: the three square buttons outside the toolbar card, hugging the window's right corner.

Hovering any button shows its Chinese name (such as "Collapse/expand left sidebar", "Minimize (to floating icon)").

What closing actually closes

Closing behaves differently per form (Maya-embedded / standalone), so never assume it quits the program:

FormWhat clicking close doesHow to reopen
Maya-embedded (opened from shelf / menu)Only the plug-in window closes; Maya and the current scene are untouched, and the AI assistant keeps following MayaOpen the plug-in again from the shelf / menu
Standalone with the AI assistant running (or starting)Collapses to a floating icon while the AI assistant and its process keep running in the backgroundSingle-click the floating icon; its right-click menu also has Open asset library
Standalone with no AI assistant runningEnds the whole plug-in processStart the plug-in again

How to undo: in the Maya-embedded edition closing only closes the window and you can reopen at any time; in the standalone edition collapsing to the floating icon is restored with a single click. The action that really ends the process is Quit in the floating icon's right-click menu — it has no protection beyond a second confirmation, so avoid it while AI tasks are running.

Beginner pitfalls

  • The left sidebar "disappeared" after collapsing: it actually became a column of icons. Hover an icon to see its name, and click it to switch to the matching tab.
  • Cannot find the window after minimizing: look for the squirrel floating icon near the bottom-right of the desktop or the taskbar and single-click it. If the window was fully covered, the icon may sit at the screen edge.
  • Closing inside Maya and assuming Maya quit: it did not; only the plug-in window closed, and Maya and the scene are still there.
  • The right sidebar will not collapse: your layout has been reduced to a single column and the plug-in refuses. Restore it with the toolbar's Panels and layout → Reset layout, see 8.2.5.
