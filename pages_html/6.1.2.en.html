One export goes from selected objects to an asset in the grid through five groups of settings: category, mode, format, naming and capture.

Prerequisites

  • Maya has the scene to export open and the plug-in main window is connected to the library.
  • A writable category is selected in the left tree. 「全部」 (All) and sub-library roots cannot hold assets directly, and a top-level category redirects to its 「自定义」 (Custom) sub-category automatically.
  • The DAG objects or material nodes to export are selected in the Maya viewport.
  • At least one content source is checked (material preset, geometry, cache, proxy, or 贴图 textures/ (textures)); with none checked the result is only meta.json and a thumbnail.

Steps

  1. Select the target sub-library and category in the left tree.
  2. Select objects in the Maya viewport; export reads the current selection live rather than the selection captured when the dialog opened.
  3. Click 「创建资产」 (Create asset) in the toolbar to open the export dialog. It is non-modal, so the rest of the interface stays usable during the export.
  4. Choose the export mode in the left column: single asset, full-auto or semi-auto; full-auto also lets you set 「截图延迟」 (Capture delay).
  5. Choose the thumbnail source in the left column: screenshot tool, Maya playblast or render.
  6. Check the export content in the right column. Check 「收集场景中已挂载的缓存/代理/引用文件」 (collect caches / proxies / references attached in the scene) when those files are needed, and keep 「贴图 textures/」 (textures) when material textures are needed.
  7. When animation is needed, choose current frame, timeline or keyframes under 「动画导出」 (Animation export).
  8. In 「命名信息」 (Naming information) fill in the name (English, used in the file name) and the display name (Chinese, optional); pick or type tags in the tag area.
  9. Click 「创建资产」 (Create asset).
  10. In screenshot-tool mode marquee a region on the screen overlay and confirm; in batch mode wait for the progress window. When it finishes the asset appears in the grid and is selected automatically.

Verify the result

  • A <name>.zasset folder appears in the category directory and the grid shows the matching card and thumbnail.
  • Opening .zasset/meta.json shows formats matching the formats actually checked.
  • After a batch export a result dialog appears: on full success it shows the count; with failures it lists the asset names and reasons plus a 「重试」 (Retry) button that reruns only the failed items.

Tips and warnings

Tip: the screenshot tool is fastest; playblast and render generate thumbnails after the whole batch finishes, without blocking the geometry export.

Tip: a same-named asset opens a conflict dialog where you can auto-rename or type a new name, and optionally remember the choice for this run.

Warning: timeline plus render renders frame by frame, so Maya becomes unresponsive for a while; this is normal, just wait for it to finish.
