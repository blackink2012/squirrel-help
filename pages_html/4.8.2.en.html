Batch delete moves the selected assets into the recycle bin, from where they can be restored through 「回收站」 (Recycle bin) on the left; still confirm the selection first, because one action affects the whole batch.

Prerequisites

  • At least two assets are selected; use the context menu for a single asset.
  • The recycle bin has room (100 GB limit; when full, permanently delete old entries in the recycle bin first).

Steps

  1. Multi-select the assets to delete in the grid.
  2. Click 「批量删除」 (Batch delete) in the batch operation bar.
  3. The confirmation lists the first 20 asset names and shows the total; the rest are summarized as "N in total".
  4. Click 「是」 (Yes) after checking, or 「否」 (No) to cancel.
  5. The plug-in moves the assets into the recycle bin one by one, clearing their favorite state and refreshing the grid and category counts.

Difference from deleting one asset

  • For a single asset use 「删除」 (Delete) in the context menu; the confirmation shows one name and lets you choose between "move to recycle bin" and "delete permanently".
  • Batch delete has one path only: into the recycle bin, with no "delete permanently" entry; to remove something for good use 「删除 → 彻底删除」 (Delete → Delete permanently) in the context menu.
  • Batch delete runs item by item; a failure in the middle does not roll back the part already deleted, and failure reasons go to the log.

Verify the result

  • The assets disappear from the current grid and the category's asset count drops.
  • The count on the 「回收站」 (Recycle bin) entry on the left grows, and the entries are visible inside.
  • Right-clicking an entry in the recycle bin and choosing 「还原」 (Restore) returns the asset to its original category, where it reappears in the grid.
警告

Batch delete goes to the recycle bin and can be restored, so unlike "delete permanently" it is reversible; but the recycle bin is limited to 100 GB and never cleans itself, so when full you must permanently delete entries one by one. "Delete permanently" is not available from this entry and cannot be undone. Still read the names and counts in the confirmation dialog carefully.

提示

Restore from the recycle bin immediately after a mistake (the whole record comes back). When unsure whether an asset is still needed, moving it to a category such as "to be sorted" is easier than deleting and restoring.
