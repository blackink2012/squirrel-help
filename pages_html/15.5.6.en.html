One-line summary: right-clicking a scenes asset card in the asset grid opens this menu. Importing a scene asset brings a whole arrangement (several objects, lights, cameras) into the current scene, and the menu is structured much like the models one.

How to open it

  1. Left Category tab → click Scenes or any category under it;
  2. In the center asset grid, right-click any scene card.

In other words the scenes and models menus are structurally identical; differences come only from which files the package actually holds.

Notes specific to this sub-library

  • Import does not clear your current scene: it merges content in. If you want only the scene asset's content, create an empty scene first.
  • Scene assets tend to be large: after importing, check the Outliner for cameras / render settings that came from the asset.
  • Format conversion does not handle textures or cameras: it only converts geometry (through Maya's native export), so do not expect everything in the .fbx afterwards.
