When checked, caches, proxies, references and volume files already attached in the scene are copied into the asset's deps/ directory, so they travel with the package when the asset is delivered.

Prerequisites

  • The selected objects carry file-based nodes, such as Alembic, gpuCache, nCache, various proxies or Maya references.
  • Those files really exist on disk; files with broken paths cannot be copied.

Steps

  1. In the right column's 「收集关联文件」 (Collect linked files) section check 收集场景中已挂载的缓存/代理/引用文件 (collect caches / proxies / references attached in the scene).
  2. Keep 贴图 textures/ (textures) when material textures are needed. Textures go into textures/, not deps/.
  3. Check the other formats as needed and click 「创建资产」 (Create asset).

Verify the result

  • .zasset/deps/ gains sub-directories by type: caches/, proxies/, references/, volumes/, textures/, audio/, movies/, imageplanes/, others/.
  • The dependency list is visible in meta.json; when everything was found dependencies_missing is empty.
  • The export result or log reports how many were collected, missing and out of scope.

Tips and warnings

Tip: scanning always runs; even with collection unchecked, missing and out-of-scope dependencies are recorded in meta.json and reported in the log. The checkbox only decides whether they are copied to disk.

Tip: frame sequences containing #, ####, <f> or <UDIM> are expanded into actual files; same-named files from different directories get a short hash appended so they do not overwrite each other.

Tip: files already copied into textures/ as textures are not stored a second time under deps/.

Warning: out-of-scope dependencies (for example shared libraries on a system or network location) are not copied and are only recorded in dependencies_out_of_scope; confirm before delivery that the recipient's environment can reach them.
