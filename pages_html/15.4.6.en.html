One-line summary: two ways to look at the same asset list — Icons is a wall of thumbnail cards, List is a table with one asset per row; switching only changes presentation, not content.

How to open it

  1. Find the view button on the right of the asset area toolbar (it shows the current mode: "Icons" means card view, "List" means table);
  2. Click it (it has a dropdown arrow) to open the view menu;
  3. Click the other item to switch.

Item-by-item details

The menu has 2 items and is either-or. The menu items carry no check mark — read the toolbar's View button text for the current mode ("Icons" = card view, "List" = table view).

Submenus

This menu has no second level (only two items, nothing with ▶).

Beginner pitfalls

  • Assuming assets became fewer after switching to list: they did not; it went from a few large cards per screen to many rows. Scroll down.
  • Cannot find a thumbnail adjustment in list view: list view has no large thumbnails, and the size slider mainly serves icon view.
  • Sorting looks different after switching views: both views share one sort setting; if it really looks wrong, check which item the Name sort menu currently has.
  • Switching views after multi-selecting: the selection follows the content, but the batch bar acts on what is currently selected, so confirm the count after switching.
