The plug-in's AI capability has three layers: local retrieval (embedding model and index), visual understanding (look at an image and suggest), and conversation plus external agents. If any layer is unavailable, browsing, importing and exporting assets are unaffected.

What works without AI

Asset browsing, categories, tags, thumbnails, import and export, and material conversion do not need a model. When semantic search is unavailable it falls back to keyword search; when AI analysis is unavailable you can fill in the name, category and tags by hand.

Notes

Checking 「AI 搜索」 (AI search) only changes how the search runs; it never uploads asset files. Whether the network is used depends on the model you choose; see "Local and cloud".
