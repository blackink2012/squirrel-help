The right column decides "what gets packaged": linked files, material and light nodes, geometry, animation, caches and proxies are all checked per section.

Plug-in status

To the right of the geometry, cache and proxy checkboxes the plug-in status is shown: 已就绪 (ready), 未加载 (not loaded) or 不可用 (unavailable). When it says unavailable the checkbox is greyed out and cannot be chosen for this export; when it says not loaded, load the plug-in in Maya's plug-in manager first and then close and reopen the export dialog.

Locked formats

Formats that do not apply to the current category are greyed out and cannot be checked; hovering says 「该格式不适用于当前分类，已锁定（可在 设置 → 导出默认值 中调整）」 (this format does not apply to the current category and is locked; adjust it in Settings → Export defaults). Default locking rules: materials and lights lock geometry and all proxy formats (such assets have no geometry); textures, HDR, images, music and animation data lock almost every node and geometry format. To unlock, turn off that format's lock on the Settings → 导出默认值 (Export defaults) page (see 6.4.1).

Hint in append-format mode

Entering from Update asset → Append format adds a yellow hint at the top of the right column: no format is checked by default, formats the asset already has are marked (已存在) (already present), checking one replaces that format's original file with this export, and leaving it unchecked keeps it as is. A normal Create asset preselects formats from the export defaults instead, which is different.

What the combinations produce

  • Only .zmetal + 贴图 textures/ (textures): a material asset whose material network is rebuilt on import.
  • A geometry format: a model asset; check .zmetal or .mcm as well when the material should travel with the model.
  • A cache or proxy: animation or renderer-specific files whose import depends on the target Maya having that plug-in installed.

Notes on the current version

Older help also drew "asset name, Chinese name and tags" on the right; in the current version they are on the left and the right column starts with collect linked files. The section names and checkbox wording in the current interface are authoritative.
