Where the toolbar is and how to use it

The toolbar is always the top strip of the window and needs no "opening". Three general rules:

  1. All buttons are icons only; hover to see the Chinese name and a description.
  2. The toolbar is also the drag area: there is a blank strip on each side of the search box, and dragging it moves the whole window; double-click the blank area to maximize or restore.
  3. Menu buttons (with a dropdown arrow) open a menu on click and never run an action directly.

Two easy misreadings

  • "Maya target" is not always there: when running inside Maya the plug-in already lives in Maya, and a standalone edition mounted by Maya has its target locked — neither form shows the button, which is normal and not a missing feature.
  • The blank space beside the search box is not a button: it is the drag area and dragging it moves the window; the search box tries to stay centered, so both blanks are always equally wide.

Beginner pitfalls

  • Clicking an icon "does nothing": buttons with ▾ only open a menu; you then click an item in that menu.
  • Cannot find "Maya target": first confirm you are in the standalone edition and did not start it from inside Maya (see the previous section).
  • The toolbar got dragged around / panel titles disappeared: the toolbar is not at fault; open 8.2.5 and click Reset layout to restore the default.
