Dragging an unselected card first selects only it and then drags it; dragging a selected card brings the whole selection.

Prerequisites

  • The asset grid holds several cards that can be dragged out of the plug-in window.
  • A Maya scene is open.

Rule

Card state while draggingWhat is actually dragged in
The card is in the selectionThe whole selection is dragged in
The card is not in the selection (something else is selected)Only the dragged card, which is selected first

This rule prevents "dragging B but importing the currently selected A".

Steps

  1. Simply hold and drag the target card; if it is not in the selection it becomes the only selection automatically.
  2. To drag several assets at once, multi-select first and then hold any one of them.
  3. Drag out of the plug-in window and release in the viewport.

Verify the result

  • The cards highlighted during the drag match the assets actually imported.
  • After a multi-selection drag the number of newly added assets equals the number selected.
  • The IDs in the Script Editor's [DragDrop] 收到拖拽: [asset IDs…] match what you expect.

Tips

  • A single click also changes the selection; confirm the highlighted range before dragging.
  • Dragging a selected card never changes the selection, and the batch imports using each asset's double-click preset format.
  • A mixed selection of materials and models imports item by item without sharing a format.
  • "Dragging selects" also holds for images and audio, but releasing over the viewport does not import them (see 5.6.1).
