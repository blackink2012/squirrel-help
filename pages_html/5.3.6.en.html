An HDR asset is used in the scene two ways: create a new dome light, or assign the HDR to an existing dome light.

Prerequisites

  • A Maya scene is open.
  • The HDR asset has .hdr or .exr files under textures/.

Steps

Create a dome light

  1. Right-click the HDR asset and expand 「创建环境光」 (Create environment light).
  2. Choose a light template; templates come from the .ma files under the plug-in's Assets/HDR_ligt/ and differ per renderer.
  3. The plug-in imports the template, prepares the HDR path per the texture import policy and replaces the template's texture attribute.

Assign to an existing dome light

  1. Select a dome light in the viewport or the Outliner.
  2. Right-click the HDR asset and choose 「指定贴图」 (Assign texture).
  3. The plug-in connects the HDR to the selected light.

Verify the result

  • A new light appears in the Outliner, or the existing light's texture attribute is updated.
  • The HDR file node's color space is Raw.
  • The environment light works in a render preview and the background shows the matching HDR content.

Tips

  • The texture import policy decides whether the HDR is copied into the project, read from the asset directory directly or keeps its source path.
  • Dragging an HDR straight into the viewport makes the plug-in silently choose a light template from the current renderer or the double-click preset, without a dialog.
  • Creating an environment light clears the selection when it finishes; select the light manually to keep adjusting it.
