Deleting a category moves it, its sub-categories and all its assets into the recycle bin, with two confirmations; a mistake can be undone by restoring the whole branch from 「回收站」 (Recycle bin) on the left.

Prerequisites

Know which category you are deleting (the whole subtree goes) and make sure the recycle bin has room (100 GB limit).

Steps

  1. Right-click the category and choose 「删除分类」 (Delete category). Core sub-libraries and the automatically created 「自定义」 (Custom) sub-category do not offer this item.
  2. First confirmation: the dialog states how many sub-categories and assets will be deleted; click 「是」 (Yes).
  3. Second, 「最终确认删除」 (Final delete confirmation): a strong warning says the category and its contents go to the recycle bin (with counts when assets exist), the focus defaults to 「否」 (No), so click 「是」 (Yes) to confirm.

Verify the result

The category disappears from the tree and the whole folder moves into the recycle bin; the parent category is selected automatically and the grid no longer shows those assets. The count on the 「回收站」 (Recycle bin) entry on the left increases.

Warnings

  • Deletion goes to the recycle bin and can be restored, but what comes back is the whole category branch, not individual assets; sub-categories and assets return together.
  • The recycle bin is limited to 100 GB and never cleans itself; once you are sure, go in and 「彻底删除」 (Delete permanently) item by item — that step cannot be undone.
  • Deleting a category while keeping its assets is not supported today: move the assets to another category first, or export a backup.
  • Do not delete category folders in Explorer; index and favorite records may be left behind. Delete inside the plug-in.
  • To be safe, export the whole library or the target assets first as described in "Backup and restore".
