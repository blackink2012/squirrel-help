「导入贴图」 (Import texture) merges the selected textures into one PBR texture asset inside the current category.

Prerequisites

  • A specific category is selected on the left. On 「全部资产」 (All assets) the whole 「从外部导入」 (Import from Files) menu is disabled and only the grey line 「当前分类不支持从外部导入」 (this category does not support importing from files) is shown.
  • The current top-level category is materials or textures: 「导入贴图」 (Import texture) only appears in 「从外部导入」 (Import from Files) of those two sub-libraries and not in the others.
  • The texture file names indicate their role (such as _basecolor, _normal, _roughness).

Steps

  1. Click 「从外部导入」 (Import from Files) in the toolbar above the asset area.
  2. Choose 「导入贴图」 (Import texture).
  3. Select several textures of one PBR set in the file dialog; png, jpg, jpeg, tga, tif, tiff, bmp and exr are supported.
  4. On confirm the plug-in creates one .zasset and puts all textures into its textures/.
  5. The asset name comes from the color texture's file name: the PBR role suffix is removed first, then resolution suffixes such as _1k, _2k and _4k.

Verify the result

  • One texture asset appears in the grid, with a thumbnail generated from the color texture.
  • textures/ in the asset directory contains every texture selected.
  • It reports 「成功导入 N 个贴图资产」 (imported N texture assets); when no color role is recognized the thumbnail uses the last file.

Tips

  • A multi-selection is treated as one PBR set; import different sets separately.
  • Role detection relies on the alias rules in Assets/preset/pbr_mapping.json; non-standard names affect channel matching.
  • After it is in the library, right-click 「创建材质」 (Create material) to connect the textures to the material channels by role.
