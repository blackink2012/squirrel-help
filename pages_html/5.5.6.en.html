「创建材质」 (Create material) builds a new material node of a chosen type from a texture asset and connects the textures automatically according to their PBR roles.

Prerequisites

  • The texture asset carries a properties record.
  • The renderer plug-in for the target material type is installed; uninstalled types are unavailable in the menu.

Steps

  1. Right-click the texture asset and expand 「创建材质」 (Create material).
  2. Choose a material type: openPBRSurface, aiStandardSurface, VRayMtl, RedshiftStandardMaterial, RedshiftOpenPBRMaterial, standardSurface, lambert and so on, as listed in material_presets in the current config.json.
  3. When the asset has several resolution directories, choose one.
  4. The plug-in creates the material node and connects base color, normal, roughness, metalness and other textures to their channels by role.

Verify the result

  • A new material node of the chosen type appears in the Hypershade.
  • Texture channels are connected and the file nodes point at the matching resolution inside the asset.
  • The Script Editor reports the material name, node type and number of textures.

Tips

  • This creates a new material and never modifies existing ones; to change the textures of an existing material use 「指定贴图」 (Assign texture).
  • The material name is the asset name plus the last four characters of the asset ID, avoiding clashes with same-named materials in the scene.
  • Creation fails when the textures do not match properties; check that the asset is a complete PBR asset.
  • Without a chosen resolution the asset's default_resolution in meta.json is used.
