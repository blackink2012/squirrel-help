Drag an asset card out of the plug-in window and release over the Maya viewport to import it; the drop semantics differ by sub-library.

Drop behavior by sub-library

Sub-libraryResult after releasing
Models / Dynamics / ScenesImports and snaps to the surface under the cursor: stands up along the normal with its bottom edge on the surface
LightsImports and aims the light direction (−Z) at the normal of the hit surface
Materials / TexturesRebuilds the material network; assigns it to the selected object when something is selected
HDRPicks a template by the current renderer and creates a dome light

Steps

  1. Hold a card in the asset grid and drag it out of the plug-in window.
  2. While dragging model-type assets the viewport shows an axis preview: the origin is the drop point and the green axis is the normal direction.
  3. Move the cursor onto the target model surface and release.
  4. With several cards selected, dragging one imports the whole batch using each asset's double-click preset; if the whole batch can snap, the assets line up side by side along the surface.

Verify the result

  • The newly imported model appears on the surface under the cursor and is selected automatically.
  • After dropping a material onto a selected object, the object's shading network is replaced or assigned.
  • The Script Editor shows a [DragDrop] 收到拖拽: [...] log line.
  • If no surface is hit, the import happens at the original position and the model still appears in the scene.

Tips

  • Snapping is controlled by Settings → 常规 → 拖放 (General → Drag and drop) and is on by default; with it off, models import at their original position.
  • Dragging selects: if you drag a card that is not currently selected it is selected first, so you never import the wrong asset.
  • A mixed selection of models and materials is imported one by one without snapping.
