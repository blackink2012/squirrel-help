Hiding a top-level category only affects the left column; it never deletes folders or assets on disk.

Prerequisites

An asset library is loaded.

Steps

  1. Open 「设置」 (Settings).
  2. Choose 「子库与分类」 (Sub-libraries and categories) on the left.
  3. Select the sub-library or top-level category to change in the list on the left.
  4. Check or uncheck 「显示在主 UI」 (Show in the main UI) at the top right. Unchecked means hidden.
  5. Click 「应用」 (Apply) or 「确定」 (OK).

Verify the result

The category tree and the collapsed icon strip rebuild immediately: the hidden top-level category no longer appears, while 「全部资产」 (All assets) is always kept. If the hidden category was selected, the selection falls back to 「全部资产」 (All assets).

Tips

  • The setting only affects the main asset library's category tree; the project library tree on the 「项目」 (Projects) tab always shows every top-level category.
  • Assets inside hidden items remain in the library and can be reached through 「全部资产」 (All assets) or search.
  • The setting is stored as hidden_top_categories (blacklist semantics); when it cannot be read, everything is shown.
  • A sub-library is also a top-level node, so uncheck 「显示在主 UI」 (Show in the main UI) for it as well to hide it; re-check it and click 「应用」 (Apply) to restore.
