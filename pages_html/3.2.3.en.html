Maximizing makes the area under the cursor fill the main window while the others are hidden; restoring brings back the previous columns and proportions.

Maximizing an area

  1. Move the mouse over the target area (no need to click inside it first).
  2. Press Ctrl + Space, or click the function button in that area's header and choose 「最大化本区域」 (Maximize this area).
  3. The other areas hide, the toolbar and status bar remain, and the target area fills the middle.

Restoring

  1. Press Ctrl + Space again.
  2. Or choose 「还原区域」 (Restore area) in the area menu.
  3. All areas reappear and the proportions return to the pre-maximize layout.

Verify the result

  • While maximized the other panels are invisible and the target panel is clearly larger.
  • After restoring, the column widths and heights are back to what they were, with the content and selection unchanged.
  • Closing and reopening the window keeps the last maximized area (the layout is saved automatically).

Tips

  • Maximizing does nothing when there is only one area.
  • The preview area is normally pinned to the height of its thumbnail; maximizing temporarily releases that, and restoring re-fits it to the content.
  • If the layout tree cannot be read (for example when an area is floating), maximizing silently does nothing; dock the floating area back first.
