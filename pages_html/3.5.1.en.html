The Help Center is a local web page: the home page uses cards as entry points, the left side holds the four-level contents of the whole book, and the article has a breadcrumb and previous / next links above it.

Opening the help

  1. Click 「帮助」 (Help) in the main window toolbar, to the left of the right-column collapse button.
  2. The plug-in starts the local help service and opens the help home page in your browser; if the Help Center is unavailable it falls back to the legacy help page.
  3. You can also reach each chapter from the cards on the help home page.

Home page

The top of the home page holds the search box and the title, followed by 8 entry cards: What's new, 5-minute start, Interface overview, Common workflows, Shortcuts, Command reference, FAQ and Support. Pages you have visited appear under 「最近访问」 (Recently visited).

Contents and article

  • The left contents tree has up to four levels: book → chapter → section → article page; click to expand or collapse.
  • Opening a page expands the tree automatically and highlights the current path.
  • A breadcrumb above the article lets you click back up level by level.
  • Long pages show 「本页内容」 (On this page) anchors on the right, highlighting the current heading as you scroll.
  • The bottom of the page has previous / next links and 「此页是否有帮助」 (Was this page helpful?).

Deep links and narrow screens

  • Every page has a stable address #/<page ID>, for example #/3.1.1, which you can bookmark or share. English pages use #/en/<page ID>.
  • On narrow windows the left contents collapse into a drawer; click the button to expand it.
  • A page ID that does not exist shows 「找不到这个帮助页面」 (Help page not found) with a link back to the home page.

Verify the result

  • Home page cards lead to the matching chapters, and the breadcrumb walks back level by level.
  • Refreshing the browser keeps you on the same page with the contents tree still expanded.
